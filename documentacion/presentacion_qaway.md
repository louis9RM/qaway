## 2 · The problem  *(tell it as a chat)*

In the Peruvian Andes, roads are cut by **landslides and river floods** after the rains.

💬 *"The road to my town is blocked again — a hillside came down."*
💬 *"And the driver never saw it coming."*

- A **driver cannot look at a map** while driving curves and fog.
- The risk data exists… but **never reaches the person driving, in time.**
- **Rural communities get isolated**: no ambulances, no supplies.

---

## 3 · Meet Qaway — what it detects

**"The one who watches" (Quechua).** Open-source web app powered by **NISAR** radar.

It detects **three road hazards** from space:
- 🏔️ **Landslides** — a slope that already moved (surface change).
- 📉 **Slow slope movement** — millimeters per week, **early warning before collapse**.
- 🌊 **River flooding / overflow** — new water rising near the road.

🛰️ Real **NISAR (NASA–ISRO)** radar — sees **at night and through clouds**. Works anywhere NISAR covers; demo in Huánuco, Peru.

---

## 4 · How does it work? — two ways to use it

*(Visual: real SCREENSHOT of Qaway — map + voice panel + the spoken result.)*

**🎙️ Mode 1 — By voice (the driver, hands-free)**
Asks *"Is my route safe?"* → Qaway takes the **GPS**, draws the **road corridor**, analyzes it, and **answers out loud**.

**✏️ Mode 2 — Draw a polygon (analyst / authority)**
Draws an area on the map → Qaway analyzes exactly that zone and reports the result.

**Both modes run the same engine:**
1. 🛰️ Downloads the latest **NISAR** passes for the area.
2. 🧠 The **AI agent decides** which analysis to run (landslide · movement · flood) and reads the real numbers.
3. 🔊 Delivers a **spoken, actionable alert** in **Spanish or English**, simple or technical.

→ Complex radar data becomes an **actionable alert** in seconds.

---

## 5 · What makes Qaway different

- 🧠 **Agentic AI by voice** — not a fixed menu; it reasons and runs the right analysis for any question.
- 🎚️ **One data source, served to each person:** citizen (simple) ↔ scientist (technical), ES / EN.
- ⏱️ **Early warning** — detects slope movement in **millimeters, weeks before collapse** (InSAR).
- 🔬 **Certified methods** (ASF/NASA, Wishart, InSAR, ARSET) — reproducible, not a black box.
