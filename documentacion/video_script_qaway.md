## Style guide
- **Tone:** cinematic, hopeful, trustworthy — "technology that saves lives."
- **Language:** English only. **Hard limit: 30 seconds.** Keep narration ~60 words total.
- **Pace:** fast, punchy, one short line per scene.
- **Palette:** Andean greens, earth tones, tech-blue radar/data beams.
- 🔬 **Scientific accuracy:** NISAR is an *active* side-looking radar — the **satellite emits** its own L-band pulses toward Earth; the echo **backscatters** back to the satellite. Heavy processing happens on the ground; the phone only downloads a tiny result. The phone never sends a signal to the satellite.

## Storyboard (5 beats, 30 seconds)

### Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:05)
**Visual:** Aerial of a foggy, winding Andean mountain road. Title flashes: **Qaway**.
**Narration:** "In the Andes, one landslide can cut off an entire community."

### Scene 2 — The driver asks (0:05–0:12)
**Visual:** Driver in a truck, hands-free, opens the Qaway app; a mic icon pulses; voice waves rise from the phone.
**Narration (driver):** "Qaway — is the road ahead safe?"

### Scene 3 — NISAR detects the change (0:12–0:22)
**Visual — KEY MOMENT:** The **NISAR satellite** flies over and **emits a blue L-band radar beam sideways/down through the clouds**; the echo bounces back up to the satellite. Two passes overlay on the ground and a **red change patch** lights up on the slope.
**Narration:** "NISAR radar sees through clouds and finds the slope has changed — a landslide warning."

### Scene 4 — The app answers (0:22–0:28)
**Visual:** The phone speaks (voice waves). A red alert zone with distance appears; the driver slows down safely.
**Narration (app voice):** "Warning: landslide zone, one kilometer ahead."

### Scene 5 — Close (0:28–0:30)
**Visual:** Pull back to mountains + satellite. Logo **Qaway** + tagline.
**Narration:** "Qaway — the radar that warns you, even with no signal."
